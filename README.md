
# 5kW Rooftop Solar Design - Accra, Ghana
**By Matilda Mireku, UMaT REED Student | 2026 | For IRENA Youth Forum 2027**

### Overview
5kW residential PV for typical 3-bedroom house in Accra. Designed to reduce ECG bills during dumsor and provide hybrid backup.
**Location:** 5.60°N, 0.21°W | **Tool:** NREL PVWatts | **Irradiance:** 5.42 kWh/m2/day

### System
- Array: 10 x 500W Mono = 5.0kW DC
- Inverter: 5kW Hybrid (Deye/Growatt) - Grid-tie + Battery ready
- Tilt 10° | Azimuth 180° South | Losses 14.08% | Roof ~30 sqm

### Performance (PVWatts Verified - 7269 kWh/year)
- Annual: 7,269 kWh/year | 19.9 kWh/day | Capacity Factor 16.6%
- Monthly Avg: 606 kWh
- Best: Nov 655 kWh (6.00) | Lowest: June 483 kWh (4.33 rainy season)

### Process
1. Load: 600-750 kWh/month for 3-bed house
2. Resource: Verified with PVWatts 5.42 kWh/m2/day
3. Sizing: 10x500W panels + 5kW hybrid inverter
4. Simulation: 7269 kWh/year yield
5. Economics: PURC tariff analysis

### Economics - Ghana Cedis
- PURC July 2026 Tariff: 2.69 GHS/kWh (301+)
- Annual Savings: ~14,810 GHS/year
- System Cost: ~56,100 GHS
- Payback: 3.8 Years
- CO2 Saved: 4.1 tons/year = 102 tons / 25 years
